seven rules the map follows
1
One way upA single route from the crack to the ledge. Each gate opens when the boss before it falls, so there is nothing to get lost in and nothing to miss.
2
The goal is always in sightTwo warm smudges high on the wall. From anywhere on the route you can see where you are going; the pressed forest far below shows how far you have come.
3
The arenas are the arenasEvery island has the game's arena layout: radius 18, the same pillars, wells, pads and crystals, the boss beyond the north edge. The fights do not change.
4
The walk is the restThe quiet beat between bosses ("the wall is quiet. it should not be.") becomes a stair you walk, with a lantern at each end.
5
The hound is a clockThe Cinderfox shadows you on the stairs and the span. It cannot be killed, only driven off; it will not enter lantern light; it stops at the foot of the last stair.
6
Help has a priceWells, pads and crystals work as they do today. The near wells are safe and small, the far ones cost a walk past the pillars.
7
The void is part of itWall, drifting rocks, glowing roots. The bridge you arrive by is the crack you fell through.
